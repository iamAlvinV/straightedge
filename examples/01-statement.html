Straightedge

Design execution engine

A design that fails a gate is wrong, not a matter of taste.

Every rule in this system is one of two things. Know which one you are looking at before you argue with it.

Gate or guide

Gate
Any line with a number, a standard name, or a measurement that must pass. Pass or fail. WCAG, Material, Core Web Vitals.
Guide
Judgment. Hierarchy tactics, palette mood, pacing, imperfection, type personality. Argue with these.

Conflict order, highest wins

When two rules disagree, the one further up this list decides. The order is fixed.

  1. Accessibility gates. Never bend.
  2. Platform hard specs. Icon sizes, safe zones, ad formats, print production.
  3. The communication goal. What the piece exists to fix.
  4. The client or house brand system. Read it before overwriting it.
  5. Convention defaults. Grid, spacing scale, measure, breakpoints.
  6. Guides. Everything that is judgment.

If a brand system pushes body text under 4.5:1, a tap target under 24px, or kills a reduced-motion fallback, the gate holds. Name the conflict. Do not ship it quietly.

Craft is decided here. Taste and direction are yours.
